What we are

A workshop that happens to award certificates.

Most institutes in Kerala teach syllabus. We teach output. A student who finishes here leaves with a folder of working projects — a disease-detection model, a farm sensor, a website a real shop uses — and the vocabulary to explain how each one was built.

That is the whole method. Sixty hours, sixty projects, six domains: medicine, agriculture, law, arts, sport and everyday life.

The method

Four things we refuse to compromise on.

01

Labs, not lectures

Machines, boards, cameras and sensors on every desk. Class time is build time; theory is what you read before you arrive.
