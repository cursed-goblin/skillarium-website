Privacy notice

Plain-language summary of what we collect through this website and why. Have your lawyer review this before launch.

What we collect

Name, phone number, email, city, educational or professional details, and any resume or marksheet you upload through the application forms. Enquiry forms collect name, phone, email and your message.

Why we collect it

Solely to assess your application, contact you about it, and administer enrolment or recruitment. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

In a private Google Sheet owned by Skillarium, with resume files in a private Google Drive folder. Neither is publicly accessible; only authorised Skillarium staff can open them.

How long we keep it

Student applications are retained for two years from submission. Faculty applications are retained for one year unless you ask us to keep them on file longer.

Your rights

Write to us to request a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. Include your application reference number so we can identify the record.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies. Any analytics used is limited to aggregate page-view measurement.
